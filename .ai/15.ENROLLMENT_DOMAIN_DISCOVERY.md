# Enrollment Domain Discovery

## Purpose

This document records the approved results of the Enrollment Domain Discovery Workshop and is the implementation-independent source for synchronizing the official project documentation.

It must be used to update, at minimum:

- `00.INDEX.md`
- `11.DOMAIN_MODEL.md`
- `12.DATABASE_DESIGN.md`
- `13.ERD.md`
- `14.CATALOGS.md`
- `20.DECISIONS.md`
- `02.CURRENT_STATUS.md`
- `03.ROADMAP.md`
- `04.DEVELOPMENT_LOG.md`

This document defines approved business behavior and tactical domain design. It does not define database tables, migrations, controllers, routes or user-interface implementation.

When translating this discovery into official artifacts:

- preserve each document's single responsibility;
- retain existing decisions that remain valid;
- replace obsolete enrollment-domain definitions;
- do not invent missing business rules;
- stop and request clarification when a required decision is not supported.

# 1. Scope

The approved scope covers:

- Representative Portal;
- representative information updates;
- family addresses;
- emergency contacts;
- authorized pickup persons;
- annual student enrollment;
- billing information;
- medical information;
- institutional transportation requirement;
- institutional-document acceptance;
- submission, reopening, completion and cancellation;
- Aggregates, Entities and Value Objects;
- Domain Services, Domain Events and Repositories;
- Bounded Contexts and Context Map.

Payment is outside the current scope.

Students do not access the system during the MVP. Only representatives access the Representative Portal.

# 2. Ubiquitous Language

| Term | Approved meaning |
|---|---|
| `Person` | Unique physical identity registered in an institution. |
| `User` | System-access account associated with a `Person`. |
| `Representative` | Institutional role through which a person represents and manages students. |
| `Student` | Stable academic role associated with a person. |
| `Family` | Administrative unit grouping representatives, students and reusable family resources. |
| `Enrollment` | Annual enrollment of one student for one academic period. |
| `FamilyAddress` | Reusable address registered within a family. |
| `EmergencyContact` | Family-owned emergency-contact data; it is not a `Person`. |
| `AuthorizedPickup` | Family-owned pickup-authorization data; it is not a `Person`. |
| `DocumentAcceptance` | Acceptance of a specific institutional-document version for an enrollment. |
| `EnrollmentSubmissionSnapshot` | Copy of selected family data captured when an enrollment is submitted. |
| `AcademicPlacement` | Grade and section assigned for one enrollment. |

The concepts `EnrollmentProcess` and `StudentEnrollment` are removed. The approved model uses one Aggregate named `Enrollment`.

# 3. Actors

## 3.1 Secretaría

Secretaría may:

- create and maintain identities and institutional roles;
- create representatives, students and families;
- associate representatives and students with families;
- maintain fields reserved for Secretaría;
- initiate annual enrollment when applicable;
- review submitted enrollments;
- reopen an enrollment for corrections;
- complete or cancel an enrollment.

## 3.2 Representative

The representative may:

- access the portal through an active `User`;
- select a family context when associated with multiple families;
- update permitted personal and contact information;
- update optional employment information;
- manage family addresses and assignments;
- manage emergency contacts and assignments;
- manage authorized pickups and assignments;
- complete annual enrollment information;
- accept required institutional documents;
- submit an enrollment.

The representative may not:

- create students or other representatives;
- access another representative's private information;
- manage students outside the selected authorized family;
- modify fields reserved for Secretaría;
- modify grade or section;
- edit after submission unless Secretaría reopens the enrollment.

## 3.3 Student

The student:

- has no portal access during the MVP;
- is managed by Secretaría and authorized representatives;
- has one active family at a time;
- has one `Enrollment` per academic period.

# 5. Cross-Cutting Business Rules

## 5.1 Automatic saving

The portal does not require a manual Save button for normal progress.

Approved behavior:

1. After a field changes, wait approximately 800–1,000 ms without additional changes and save pending values.
2. Before moving between sections or students, immediately save pending values.
3. Attempt to save pending changes before page exit when technically possible, but never depend exclusively on browser-exit behavior.

Visible states:

```text
Saving...
Saved
Save error
```

When saving fails:

- entered data remains visible;
- the application retries when appropriate;
- the representative is warned before leaving if pending data could not be saved.

Autosave is an application/persistence concern and does not emit a Domain Event for each changed field.

## 5.2 Free navigation

In `Draft`, the representative may advance, go back, leave, return later and modify any permitted section.

## 5.3 Section completion

Each section may be presented as `Pending` or `Complete`. Incomplete sections do not block navigation, but submission requires all mandatory validations.

## 5.4 Intelligent preloading

For a new academic period:

- reuse current `Person`, `Representative` and `Student` data;
- keep existing family addresses, emergency contacts and authorized pickups available;
- optionally preload approved annual data from the previous `Enrollment`;
- never modify the previous enrollment.

Annual data eligible for preloading:

- billing information;
- medical information;
- transportation requirement;
- authorization to leave alone.

Family resources remain in `Family` during `Draft` and are copied only into the submission snapshot.

## 5.5 Referential integrity

An assigned family resource cannot be deleted before it is unassigned. Deactivation may be used when historical integrity requires preservation.

## 5.6 Catalogs

Institution-managed configurable values come from Catalogs. `Occupation` is free text, not a catalog.

## 5.7 Access and isolation

A representative may only manage his or her own information, authorized students and resources of the selected family.

Every server request validates:

```text
User
→ Person
→ Representative
→ FamilyRepresentative
→ selected Family
```

Client-side selection alone is never authorization.

# 6. Data Ownership

Approved rule:

> Data describing a person belongs to `Person` or its role. Data describing one academic year's enrollment belongs to `Enrollment`. Reusable household resources belong to `Family`.

## 6.1 Person

Owns:

- names;
- identification;
- birth date;
- sex;
- marital status when applicable;
- education level when applicable;
- personal email;
- mobile phone;
- landline phone.

A `Person` may exist without `ContactInformation` and without `User`.

## 6.2 Representative

Owns:

- `PersonId`;
- optional employment information;
- representative status.

## 6.3 Student

Owns:

- `PersonId`;
- institutional code;
- admission date;
- student status.

Grade and section do not belong to `Student`.

## 6.4 Family

Owns:

- family memberships;
- addresses and assignments;
- emergency contacts and assignments;
- authorized pickups and assignments.

`EmergencyContact` and `AuthorizedPickup` are not `Person`.

## 6.5 Enrollment

Owns:

- academic period and placement;
- billing information;
- medical information;
- transportation requirement;
- authorization to leave alone;
- document acceptances;
- status and lifecycle timestamps;
- submission snapshot.

- family retains at least one active representative;
- only one active primary representative per family;
- deactivation does not delete representative or person;
- active relation is required for access.

When a representative has one active family, enter it directly. With multiple active families, show a family selector and a visible `Change family` option.

## 11.6 AddressAssignment

Conceptually:

```text
AddressAssignment
├── AddressAssignmentId
├── FamilyAddressId
├── MemberType
├── MemberId
└── Status
```

Supported member types: `Representative`, `Student`.

Rules:

- a family may register multiple addresses;
- an address may serve multiple members;
- each representative has at most one active address within a family;
- each student has at most one active address;
- assigning a new address replaces/deactivates the previous active assignment;
- address and member belong to the same family;
- a representative may assign only to self and related students;
- a representative cannot modify another representative's address;
- an assigned address cannot be deleted.

The physical model must avoid unsafe polymorphic foreign keys. Separate representative-address and student-address relations are acceptable while preserving this conceptual responsibility.

7. Family resources may be reused across siblings.
8. Assigned resources cannot be deleted before unassignment.
9. Representatives cannot access another representative's private information.
10. Every operation validates family context server-side.

# 12. Aggregate: Enrollment

## 12.1 Definition

> Annual enrollment of one student for one academic period.

```text
1 Student + 1 AcademicPeriod = 1 Enrollment
```

Each sibling has a separate enrollment.

## 12.2 Structure

```text
Enrollment
├── EnrollmentId
├── StudentId
├── FamilyId
├── AcademicPeriodId
├── Status
├── AcademicPlacement
├── BillingInformation
├── MedicalInformation
├── TransportInformation
├── DocumentAcceptances
├── IsAuthorizedToLeaveAlone
├── EnrollmentSubmissionSnapshot?
├── StartedAt
├── SubmittedAt?
├── CompletedAt?
└── CancelledAt?
```

Only one active enrollment may exist for `StudentId + AcademicPeriodId`, with institution scoping applied consistently.

## 12.3 States

```text
Draft
Submitted
Completed
Cancelled
```

Transitions:

```text
Draft ─────────► Submitted
Submitted ─────► Completed
Submitted ─────► Draft       reopened by Secretaría
Draft ─────────► Cancelled
Submitted ─────► Cancelled
```

`Draft`: editable, autosaved, freely navigable, may be incomplete.

`Submitted`: locked for the representative and available for administrative review.

`Completed`: confirmed by Secretaría and not editable.

`Cancelled`: inactive and not editable.

Previous states `Returned`, `Approved` and `Rejected` are obsolete.

## 12.4 AcademicPlacement

```text
AcademicPlacement
├── GradeId
└── SectionId?
```

Rules:

- system proposes next grade;
- Secretaría may modify it;
- representative can view but not modify;
- section may be assigned later;
- both belong to `Enrollment`.

No detailed promotion algorithm is approved beyond proposing the next grade and allowing administrative override.

## 12.5 BillingInformation

```text
BillingInformation
├── IdentificationTypeId
├── IdentificationNumber
├── LegalName
├── BillingAddress
├── BillingEmail
└── Phone
```

All fields are required before submission. Billing address is independent of the active family/student address.

## 12.6 MedicalInformation

```text
MedicalInformation
├── HasMedicalCondition
├── MedicalConditionDetail?
├── HasAllergies
├── AllergyDetail?
├── TakesPermanentMedication
├── MedicationName?
├── RequiresSpecialCare
├── SpecialCareDetail?
├── HasMedicalInsurance
├── InsuranceProvider?
├── PediatricianName?
├── PediatricianPhone?
└── Observations?
```

Conditional rules:

- medical condition `true` requires detail;
- allergies `true` requires detail;
- permanent medication `true` requires medication name;
- dose and frequency are outside scope;
- special care `true` requires detail;
- medical insurance `true` requires insurer name;
- policy number is outside scope;
- pediatrician fields and observations are optional;
- dependent fields remain hidden/disabled until the answer is `true`.

Medical information is annual and belongs to `Enrollment`.

## 12.7 TransportInformation

```text
TransportInformation
└── RequiresInstitutionalTransport
```

Mandatory Yes/No. Route, provider, driver and transport observations are outside scope.

## 12.8 DocumentAcceptance

```text
DocumentAcceptance
├── DocumentAcceptanceId
├── InstitutionalDocumentVersionId
├── AcceptedByRepresentativeId
└── AcceptedAt
```

Rules:

- references a specific version;
- one acceptance per document version per enrollment;
- all required documents must be accepted before submission;
- one representative action covering several children creates an acceptance in each covered enrollment;
- a new required version requires a new acceptance.

A boolean is insufficient.

## 12.9 Authorization to leave alone

`IsAuthorizedToLeaveAlone` belongs to `Enrollment`.

Submission requires at least one assigned authorized pickup or `IsAuthorizedToLeaveAlone = true`.

## 12.10 EnrollmentSubmissionSnapshot

Cardinality:

```text
Enrollment 1 ─── 0..1 EnrollmentSubmissionSnapshot
```

```text
EnrollmentSubmissionSnapshot
├── EnrollmentSubmissionSnapshotId
├── EnrollmentId
├── StudentAddress
├── EmergencyContacts
├── AuthorizedPickups
├── CreatedAt
└── CreatedByRepresentativeId
```

```text
SubmittedAddressSnapshot
├── Label
├── Province
├── Canton
├── Parish
├── MainStreet
├── StreetNumber?
├── SecondaryStreet?
├── Sector?
├── Reference?
└── Geolocation?
```

```text
SubmittedEmergencyContactSnapshot
├── Names
├── Relationship
├── MobilePhone
├── Phone?
├── Email?
└── Observations?
```

```text
SubmittedAuthorizedPickupSnapshot
├── Names
├── Relationship
├── MobilePhone
├── Phone?
├── DocumentNumber
└── Observations?
```

Rules:

- no snapshot is required in `Draft`;
- first submission creates it;
- it is immutable while submitted/completed;
- reopening does not create a second enrollment;
- resubmission replaces the single snapshot with current family data;
- multiple historical snapshots are not retained within the same academic period;
- one snapshot per enrollment/period is sufficient.

The snapshot does not duplicate enrollment-owned billing, medical, transport, leave-alone, placement or acceptance data.

## 12.11 Submission validation

Submission requires:

1. Enrollment is `Draft`.
2. Representative is authorized for selected family.
3. Student actively belongs to selected family.
4. Enrollment matches student, family and academic period.
5. Required representative personal information is complete.
6. Required representative contact information is complete.
7. Required student information is complete.
8. Student has one active address.
9. Billing information is complete.
10. Medical conditional validations pass.
11. Transportation requirement is answered.
12. At least one emergency contact is assigned.
13. At least one authorized pickup is assigned or leave-alone is true.
14. All required document versions are accepted.
15. Grade is defined.
16. All mandatory business validations pass.

Submission is atomic; partial submission is prohibited. The interface warns that submission locks editing.

## 12.12 Reopening, completion and cancellation

Reopening:

```text
Submitted → Draft
```

- only Secretaría;
- representative regains edit access;
- enrollment must be submitted again;
- no new enrollment is created;
- resubmission replaces the single snapshot.

Completion:

```text
Submitted → Completed
```

Cancellation may occur from `Draft` or `Submitted` according to administrative permissions and does not delete historical records.

# 15. Domain Services

## 15.1 EnrollmentSubmissionService

```text
EnrollmentSubmissionService.Submit(
    Enrollment,
    Representative,
    Student,
    Family,
    RequiredDocuments,
    SubmittedAt
)
```

Responsibilities:

1. Validate representative-family access.
2. Validate student-family membership.
3. Validate enrollment identity and period.
4. Validate required `Person`, `Representative` and `Student` data.
5. Validate active student address.
6. Validate emergency contacts.
7. Validate pickup or leave-alone rule.
8. Validate enrollment-owned information.
9. Validate required document acceptances.
10. Build or replace snapshot.
11. Transition to `Submitted`.

## 15.2 EnrollmentInitializationService

```text
EnrollmentInitializationService.Start(
    Student,
    Family,
    AcademicPeriod,
    PreviousEnrollment?
)
```

Responsibilities:

- ensure no enrollment exists for student/period;
- propose next grade;
- preload approved annual information;
- create `Draft`;
- record active family;
- leave previous enrollment unchanged.

## 15.3 FamilyAccessService

```text
FamilyAccessService.GetAccessibleFamilies(RepresentativeId)
FamilyAccessService.CanManageFamily(RepresentativeId, FamilyId)
```

Responsibilities:

- list accessible active families;
- direct entry for one family;
- selector for multiple families;
- reject unauthorized access;
- validate context server-side.

Authentication and HTTP authorization remain application/infrastructure responsibilities.

# 20. Bounded Contexts

Approved MVP contexts:

```text
Identity & Access
Family Management
Academic Core
Enrollment
Catalogs
Institutional Documents
```

The previous `Student Profile` context is removed.

## Identity & Access

Owns personal identity, contact information, user accounts, authentication and access state.

Principal concepts: `Person`, `User`.

## Family Management

Owns representative role, family membership, active student-family association and reusable family resources.

Principal Aggregates: `Representative`, `Family`.

## Academic Core

Owns stable student role.

Principal Aggregate: `Student`.

Does not own grade, section, medical, transport, contacts, pickups or annual enrollment data.

## Enrollment

Owns annual enrollment lifecycle and annual data, placement, document acceptance and snapshot.

Principal Aggregate: `Enrollment`.

## Catalogs

Owns institution-managed configurable values and geographic/academic hierarchies.

## Institutional Documents

Owns documents, versions and period requirements. `Enrollment` owns acceptance only.

# 22. Principal Approved Decisions

These decisions must be traceable in `20.DECISIONS.md`:

1. `Enrollment` is one student's annual enrollment for one academic period.
2. `EnrollmentProcess` and `StudentEnrollment` are removed.
3. Payment is outside the enrollment MVP.
4. Only representatives access the Representative Portal in the MVP.
5. `Person` owns identity and personal contact information.
6. `ContactInformation` is optional generically but representative email/mobile are required for submission.
7. `User` is optional for `Person`; students have no user in the MVP.
8. `Representative` is an independent role referencing `Person`.
9. Employment information belongs to `Representative` and is optional.
10. `Occupation` is free text.
11. A representative may belong to multiple active families.
12. Multiple-family representatives select an active family context after login.
13. Family access is validated server-side on every request.
14. A student has one active family at a time.
15. Historical student-family membership may be retained.
16. Family owns reusable addresses and assignments.
17. Each representative has at most one active address per family.
18. Each student has at most one active address.
19. Emergency contacts are family-owned data, not `Person`.
20. Authorized pickups are family-owned data, not `Person`.
21. Contacts and pickups can be reused across siblings.
22. Billing, medical, transport and leave-alone data belong to annual `Enrollment`.
23. Grade and section belong to `Enrollment`, not `Student`.
24. States are `Draft`, `Submitted`, `Completed`, `Cancelled`.
25. Secretaría may reopen `Submitted` to `Draft`.
26. Submission locks representative editing.
27. Document acceptance references a specific version.
28. A student may have zero pickups when authorized to leave alone.
29. Each enrollment has at most one submission snapshot for the period.
30. First submission creates the snapshot.
31. Resubmission after reopening replaces the single snapshot.
32. Multiple historical snapshots are not retained in one period.
33. `Student Profile` Bounded Context is removed.
34. Six MVP Bounded Contexts are approved.
35. The MVP remains a modular monolith.
36. Modules integrate through explicit contracts and identifiers.
37. Domain Services are limited to cross-Aggregate rules.
38. Repositories exist per Aggregate Root.
39. Autosave does not generate field-level Domain Events.

Closely related decisions may be grouped into coherent ADRs without losing traceability.
