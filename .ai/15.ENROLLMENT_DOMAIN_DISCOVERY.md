# Enrollment Domain Discovery

## Purpose

This document records the approved, implementation-independent business and tactical decisions of the Enrollment Domain Discovery Workshop.

It defines approved business behavior, language, ownership, workflows, invariants, Aggregate boundaries and Bounded Context relationships. It does not define SQL tables, column types, indexes, foreign-key strategies, framework paths, routes or deployment mechanisms.

## Discovery completion criteria

The discovery is complete when:

- every business concept has one approved meaning;
- data ownership is explicit;
- actors, workflows, invariants and lifecycle transitions are approved;
- Aggregate and Bounded Context boundaries are sufficiently defined;
- unresolved business questions are listed explicitly;
- no downstream document must invent a business rule.

---

# 1. Scope

The approved scope covers:

- Representative Portal;
- representative information updates;
- family addresses;
- emergency contacts;
- authorized pickup persons;
- annual student enrollment;
- billing information;
- medical information;
- institutional transportation requirement;
- institutional acknowledgements by Representative and AcademicPeriod;
- submission, reopening, completion and cancellation;
- Aggregates, Entities and Value Objects;
- Domain Services, Domain Events and Repositories;
- Bounded Contexts and Context Map.

Payment is outside the current scope.

Students do not access the system during the MVP. Only representatives access the Representative Portal.

# 2. Ubiquitous Language

| Term | Approved meaning |
|---|---|
| `Person` | Unique physical identity registered in an institution. |
| `User` | System-access account associated with a `Person`. |
| `Representative` | Institutional role through which a person represents and manages students. |
| `Student` | Stable academic role associated with a person. |
| `Family` | Administrative unit grouping representatives, students and reusable family resources. |
| `Enrollment` | Annual enrollment of one student for one academic period. |
| `FamilyAddress` | Reusable address registered within a family. |
| `EmergencyContact` | Family-owned emergency-contact data; it is not a `Person`. |
| `AuthorizedPickup` | Family-owned pickup-authorization data; it is not a `Person`. |
| `AcknowledgementRequirement` | Configurable institutional content reference that a Representative may be required to review for an AcademicPeriod. |
| `RepresentativeAcknowledgementCompletion` | Historical fact that one Representative completed Institutional Acknowledgements for one AcademicPeriod. |
| `RepresentativeAcknowledgement` | One requirement included in the atomic confirmation that produced a completion. |
| `EnrollmentSubmissionSnapshot` | Copy of selected family data captured when an enrollment is submitted. |
| `AcademicPlacement` | Grade and section assigned for one enrollment. |

The concepts `EnrollmentProcess` and `StudentEnrollment` are removed. The approved model uses one Aggregate named `Enrollment`.

# 3. Actors

## 3.1 Secretaría

Secretaría may:

- create and maintain identities and institutional roles;
- create representatives, students and families;
- associate representatives and students with families;
- provision a User for an existing Representative and establish or replace that User's password through the authorized administrative boundary;
- assist a Representative who cannot access the SIS by verifying identity externally, using the existing administrative password replacement and delivering the new credential outside the SIS;
- maintain fields reserved for Secretaría;
- list, create, edit, activate and deactivate acknowledgement requirements within the effective AcademicPeriod administrative context;
- initiate annual enrollment when applicable;
- review submitted enrollments;
- reopen an enrollment for corrections;
- complete or cancel an enrollment.

## 3.2 Representative

The representative may:

- access the portal through an active `User`;
- select a family context when associated with multiple families;
- update permitted personal and contact information;
- update optional employment information;
- manage family addresses and assignments;
- manage emergency contacts and assignments;
- manage authorized pickups and assignments;
- complete annual enrollment information;
- review and acknowledge the configured institutional requirements applicable to an AcademicPeriod;
- submit an enrollment.

The representative may not:

- create students or other representatives;
- access another representative's private information;
- manage students outside the selected authorized family;
- modify fields reserved for Secretaría;
- modify grade or section;
- edit after submission unless Secretaría reopens the enrollment.

## 3.3 Student

The student:

- has no portal access during the MVP;
- is managed by Secretaría and authorized representatives;
- has one active family at a time;
- has one `Enrollment` per academic period.

# 4. Representative Portal Workflow

```text
1. Start
   ├── Process summary
   └── Institutional acknowledgements

2. Representative update
   ├── Personal information
   ├── Contact information
   ├── Address
   └── Employment information

3. For each student
   ├── Personal information
   ├── Address
   ├── Billing information
   ├── Medical information
   ├── Transportation
   ├── Emergency contacts
   └── Authorized pickups / authorization to leave alone

4. Review

5. Submission
```

While the enrollment is in `Draft`, navigation between sections is free.

The Representative Portal is a permanent family self-service capability. Enrollment is a temporary process within that Portal: completing Enrollment does not end Representative access, convert Family Resources into Enrollment-owned data or prevent later authorized maintenance of current information. A Submission snapshot preserves the values sent and is not rewritten by later Family Resource changes. Rules for which personal fields may later require Secretaría approval remain outside the approved scope.

# 5. Cross-Cutting Business Rules

## 5.1 Automatic saving

The portal does not require a manual Save button for normal progress.

Approved behavior:

1. After a field changes, wait approximately 800–1,000 ms without additional changes and save pending values.
2. Before moving between sections or students, immediately save pending values.
3. Attempt to save pending changes before page exit when technically possible, but never depend exclusively on browser-exit behavior.

Visible states:

```text
Saving...
Saved
Save error
```

When saving fails:

- entered data remains visible;
- the application retries when appropriate;
- the representative is warned before leaving if pending data could not be saved.

Autosave is an application/persistence concern and does not emit a Domain Event for each changed field.

## 5.2 Free navigation

In `Draft`, the representative may advance, go back, leave, return later and modify any permitted section.

## 5.3 Section completion

Each section may be presented as `Pending` or `Complete`. Incomplete sections do not block navigation, but submission requires all mandatory validations.

## 5.4 Intelligent preloading

For a new academic period:

- reuse current `Person`, `Representative` and `Student` data;
- keep existing family addresses, emergency contacts and authorized pickups available;
- optionally preload approved annual data from the previous `Enrollment`;
- never modify the previous enrollment.

Annual data eligible for preloading:

- billing information;
- medical information;
- transportation requirement;
- authorization to leave alone.

Family resources remain in `Family` during `Draft` and are copied only into the submission snapshot.

## 5.5 Referential integrity

An assigned family resource cannot be deleted before it is unassigned. Deactivation may be used when historical integrity requires preservation.

## 5.6 Catalogs

Institution-managed configurable values come from Catalogs. `Occupation` is free text, not a catalog.

## 5.7 Access and isolation

A representative may only manage his or her own information, authorized students and resources of the selected family.

Every server request validates:

```text
User
→ Person
→ Representative
→ active FamilyRepresentative
→ selected Family
```

The User must be enabled under the existing Identity & Access rules. The resolved Person must own the Representative role, and the Representative must have an active membership in every effective Family context. Client-side selection alone is never authorization. One active Family permits direct entry; multiple active Families require selection from the authorized set and later changes only within that set.

# 6. Data Ownership

Approved rule:

> Data describing a person belongs to `Person` or its role. Data describing one academic year's enrollment belongs to `Enrollment`. Reusable household resources belong to `Family`.

## 6.1 Person

Owns:

- names;
- identification;
- birth date;
- sex;
- marital status when applicable;
- education level when applicable;
- personal email;
- mobile phone;
- landline phone.

A `Person` may exist without `ContactInformation` and without `User`.

## 6.2 Representative

Owns:

- `PersonId`;
- optional employment information;
- representative status.

## 6.3 Student

Owns:

- `PersonId`;
- institutional code;
- admission date;
- student status.

Grade and section do not belong to `Student`.

## 6.4 Family

Owns:

- family memberships;
- addresses and assignments;
- emergency contacts and assignments;
- authorized pickups and assignments.

`EmergencyContact` and `AuthorizedPickup` are not `Person`.

## 6.5 Enrollment

Owns:

- academic period and placement;
- billing information;
- medical information;
- transportation requirement;
- authorization to leave alone;
- status and lifecycle timestamps;
- submission snapshot.

## 6.6 Institutional Acknowledgements

Owns configurable `AcknowledgementRequirement` resources by AcademicPeriod and the historical `RepresentativeAcknowledgementCompletion` with its individual acknowledgements. These facts belong to Representative + AcademicPeriod, not to Student, Enrollment, Reopening or Resubmission.

## 8.4 User

`User` is an independent Aggregate Root in Identity & Access that references exactly one `Person`.

```text
User
├── UserId
├── PersonId
├── LoginIdentifier
├── PasswordHash
├── UserStatus
├── LastAccessAt?
├── FailedLoginAttempts
└── LockedAt?
```

Rules:

- `UserId` is the stable technical User identity and never depends on personal identification;
- a `Person` may exist without `User`;
- a `User` cannot exist without exactly one `Person`;
- credentials, access state and lock lifecycle belong to `User`, not `Person`;
- for a Representative User, `LoginIdentifier` is the current `DocumentNumber` of the referenced Person and is the visible username for authentication;
- a Person with a Representative User must retain complete Identification: `DocumentTypeId` and `DocumentNumber` cannot be removed or left incomplete;
- changing that Person's `DocumentNumber` changes `LoginIdentifier` to the same value in one atomic cross-Aggregate operation while preserving UserId, PersonId, PasswordHash, UserStatus, failed-login state and relationships;
- the synchronized login identifier remains globally unique within the institutional database; understandable prevalidation does not replace the physical UNIQUE guarantee;
- the synchronization belongs to Application using one outer transaction and explicit User behavior, never to Controller SQL, Person persistence or a direct repository update that bypasses User;
- students do not have a `User` or portal in the MVP;
- representatives access the portal through an enabled `User` and an active FamilyRepresentative membership.

Secretaría provisions a Representative User only when Person and Representative exist, Representative references that Person, the Person has complete Identification and a valid personal contact email, the Person has no User and DocumentNumber is available as LoginIdentifier. The database generates UserId, USER_STATUS supplies the approved state and persistence stores only a secure PasswordHash.

For initial or administrative replacement credentials, Secretaría supplies the plain-text password only during the operation. The minimum is five characters; letters and/or numbers are permitted without requiring a mixture, uppercase or symbols. The old password is not required for an administrative replacement, and no mandatory first-login change applies. The SIS never stores, returns, emails, reports or exports a readable password. Credential delivery occurs through a process external to the SIS.

No activation token, activation link, activation email, first-login activation or mandatory first-login password change belongs to the MVP. Representative Forgot Password is assisted by Secretaría: the public SIS displays one generic instruction to contact the administration office, requests no account data and performs no lookup or state change. Secretaría verifies identity externally, uses the existing administrative password replacement and delivers the new credential outside the SIS. The MVP creates no recovery email, token, OTP, reset request, expiration or autonomous reset endpoint.

2. A student has one active family.

# 12. Aggregate: Enrollment

## 12.1 Definition

> Annual enrollment of one student for one academic period.

```text
1 Student + 1 AcademicPeriod = 1 Enrollment
```

Each sibling has a separate enrollment.

## 12.2 Structure

```text
Enrollment
├── EnrollmentId
├── StudentId
├── FamilyId
├── AcademicPeriodId
├── Status
├── AcademicPlacement
├── BillingInformation
├── MedicalInformation
├── TransportInformation
├── IsAuthorizedToLeaveAlone
├── EnrollmentSubmissionSnapshot?
├── StartedAt
├── SubmittedAt?
├── CompletedAt?
└── CancelledAt?
```

Only one active enrollment may exist for `StudentId + AcademicPeriodId`, with institution scoping applied consistently.

## 12.3 States

```text
Draft
Submitted
Completed
Cancelled
```

Transitions:

```text
Draft ─────────► Submitted
Submitted ─────► Completed
Submitted ─────► Draft       reopened by Secretaría
Draft ─────────► Cancelled
Submitted ─────► Cancelled
```

`Draft`: editable, autosaved, freely navigable, may be incomplete.

`Submitted`: locked for the representative and available for administrative review.

`Completed`: confirmed by Secretaría and not editable.

`Cancelled`: inactive and not editable.

Previous states `Returned`, `Approved` and `Rejected` are obsolete.

## 12.4 AcademicPlacement

```text
AcademicPlacement
├── GradeId
└── SectionId?
```

Rules:

- system proposes next grade;
- Secretaría may modify it;
- representative can view but not modify;
- section may be assigned later;
- both belong to `Enrollment`.

No detailed promotion algorithm is approved beyond proposing the next grade and allowing administrative override.

## 12.6 MedicalInformation

```text
├── MedicalConditionDetail?
├── HasAllergies
├── AllergyDetail?
```

Conditional rules:

- special care `true` requires detail;
- medical insurance `true` requires insurer name;
- policy number is outside scope;

- dependent fields remain hidden/disabled until the answer is `true`.

Medical information is annual and belongs to `Enrollment`.

## 12.7 TransportInformation

```text
TransportInformation
└── RequiresInstitutionalTransport
```

Mandatory Yes/No. Route, provider, driver and transport observations are outside scope.

## 12.8 Institutional Acknowledgement prerequisite

Enrollment does not own Institutional Acknowledgements and contains no `DocumentAcceptance`. When a future Enrollment flow needs this prerequisite, it consults an explicit Application boundary using the authenticated Representative and the Enrollment AcademicPeriod.

The stage is satisfied when the AcademicPeriod has zero applicable active requirements or the Representative already has a `RepresentativeAcknowledgementCompletion` for that period. If applicable requirements exist and no completion exists, the stage is not satisfied. Enrollment does not inspect acknowledgement rows or recalculate a historical Completion against the current active set.

## 12.9 Authorization to leave alone

`IsAuthorizedToLeaveAlone` belongs to `Enrollment`.

Submission requires at least one assigned authorized pickup or `IsAuthorizedToLeaveAlone = true`.


```text

SubmittedAuthorizedPickupSnapshot
├── Names
```

Rules:

- no snapshot is required in `Draft`;
- first submission creates it;
- it is immutable while submitted/completed;
- reopening does not create a second enrollment;
- resubmission replaces the single snapshot with current family data;
- multiple historical snapshots are not retained within the same academic period;
- one snapshot per enrollment/period is sufficient.

The snapshot does not duplicate enrollment-owned billing, medical, transport, leave-alone or placement data, nor Representative acknowledgement facts.

## 12.11 Submission validation

Submission requires:

1. Enrollment is `Draft`.
2. Representative is authorized for selected family.
3. Student actively belongs to selected family.
4. Enrollment matches student, family and academic period.
5. Required representative personal information is complete.
6. Required representative contact information is complete.
7. Required student information is complete.
8. Student has one active address.
9. Billing information is complete.
10. Medical conditional validations pass.
11. Transportation requirement is answered.
12. At least one emergency contact is assigned.
13. At least one authorized pickup is assigned or leave-alone is true.
14. The Institutional Acknowledgements stage is satisfied through its explicit Application boundary.
15. Grade is defined.
16. All mandatory business validations pass.

Submission is atomic; partial submission is prohibited. The interface warns that submission locks editing.

## 12.12 Reopening, completion and cancellation

Reopening:

```text
Submitted → Draft
```

- only Secretaría;
- representative regains edit access;
- enrollment must be submitted again;
- no new enrollment is created;
- resubmission replaces the single snapshot.

Completion:

```text
Submitted → Completed
```

Cancellation may occur from `Draft` or `Submitted` according to administrative permissions and does not delete historical records.

# 13. Institutional Acknowledgements

The existing logical `institutional_documents` Bounded Context is retained as a technical boundary, but its approved responsibility is Institutional Acknowledgements rather than document management.

## 13.1 AcknowledgementRequirement

```text
AcknowledgementRequirement
├── AcknowledgementRequirementId
├── AcademicPeriodId
├── Title
├── URL
├── OfficialReference?
└── Status
```

Rules:

- each institution configures zero or more requirements for an AcademicPeriod without hard-coded documents or a document-type catalog;
- Title and URL are required; OfficialReference is optional; Status uses `GENERAL_STATUS`;
- the SIS stores only the configured external URL and does not store files, content, hashes, versions, publication records or proof of the exact content displayed;
- before the first acknowledgement of a requirement, Secretaría may change Title, URL, OfficialReference and Status;
- after at least one acknowledgement exists, Title and OfficialReference are immutable, while URL and Status may change;
- INACTIVE requirements are excluded from new confirmations without deleting or invalidating history or an existing Completion;
- hard delete is not part of the approved lifecycle.

Secretaría will later manage requirements through a simple AcademicPeriod-scoped capability that lists, creates, edits, activates and deactivates them. AcademicPeriod comes from trusted administrative context rather than unnecessary browser authority. No such UI is implemented by this phase.

## 13.2 RepresentativeAcknowledgementCompletion

```text
RepresentativeAcknowledgementCompletion
├── RepresentativeAcknowledgementCompletionId
├── RepresentativeId
├── AcademicPeriodId
├── CompletedAt
└── RepresentativeAcknowledgements
```

`RepresentativeAcknowledgementCompletion` is an Aggregate Root that protects the atomic confirmed set. At most one Completion exists for Representative + AcademicPeriod. Its internal `RepresentativeAcknowledgement` rows each reference one requirement included in that operation, and the same requirement cannot appear twice in one Completion.

The Representative experience presents one checkbox and external link per currently applicable active requirement and one button for the complete set. Every presented requirement must be checked. Application resolves the applicable set again on the server and creates the Completion plus all individual acknowledgements atomically; the browser never determines the authoritative required set.

Zero applicable requirements is valid and the stage is satisfied without creating an artificial Completion. When a Completion exists, it remains valid if requirements are later added or deactivated, a URL changes or period configuration changes. There is no automatic reconfirmation, expiration within the period, Student/Enrollment-specific Completion, or new Completion after Reopening or Resubmission.

An acknowledgement records awareness/review of configured institutional content. It is not by itself a contractual signature, legal acceptance, electronic signature, substitute digital consent or evidence of the exact external content viewed.

Grade, Section and AcademicPeriod belong to Academic Core. Their persistence may use reference tables, but they are not homogeneous Catalogs-owned concepts.

# 20. Bounded Contexts

Approved MVP contexts:

```text
Identity & Access
Family Management
Academic Core
Enrollment
Catalogs
Institutional Acknowledgements
```

The previous `Student Profile` context is removed.

## Identity & Access

Owns personal identity, contact information, user accounts, authentication and access state.

Principal Aggregates: `Person`, `User`.

## Family Management

Owns representative role, family membership, active student-family association and reusable family resources.

Principal Aggregates: `Representative`, `Family`.

## Academic Core

Owns stable student role, AcademicPeriod lifecycle and the server-side operational AcademicPeriod boundary.

Principal Aggregates: `Student`, `AcademicPeriod`.

Does not own medical, transport, contacts, pickups or annual enrollment data. AcademicPeriod dates describe the cycle but do not infer ACTIVE state.

## Enrollment

Owns annual enrollment lifecycle and annual data, placement and snapshot.

Principal Aggregate: `Enrollment`.

## Catalogs

Owns controlled general and geographic reference values. It does not own AcademicPeriod, Grade or Section.

## Institutional Acknowledgements

Owns configurable requirements by AcademicPeriod and Representative completion facts. It publishes only explicit Application contracts; Enrollment owns neither requirements nor acknowledgements.

# 22. Principal Approved Decisions

These decisions must be traceable in `20.DECISIONS.md`:

1. `Enrollment` is one student's annual enrollment for one academic period.
2. `EnrollmentProcess` and `StudentEnrollment` are removed.
3. Payment is outside the enrollment MVP.
4. Only representatives access the Representative Portal in the MVP.
5. `Person` owns identity and personal contact information.
6. `ContactInformation` is optional generically but representative email/mobile are required for submission.
7. `User` is optional for `Person`; students have no user in the MVP.
8. `Representative` is an independent role referencing `Person`.
9. Employment information belongs to `Representative` and is optional.
10. `Occupation` is free text.
11. A representative may belong to multiple active families.
12. Multiple-family representatives select an active family context after login.
13. Family access is validated server-side on every request.
14. A student has one active family at a time.
15. Historical student-family membership may be retained.
16. Family owns reusable addresses and assignments.
17. Each representative has at most one active address per family.
18. Each student has at most one active address.
19. Emergency contacts are family-owned data, not `Person`.
20. Authorized pickups are family-owned data, not `Person`.
21. Contacts and pickups can be reused across siblings.
22. Billing, medical, transport and leave-alone data belong to annual `Enrollment`.
23. Grade and section belong to `Enrollment`, not `Student`.
24. States are `Draft`, `Submitted`, `Completed`, `Cancelled`.
25. Secretaría may reopen `Submitted` to `Draft`.
26. Submission locks representative editing.
27. Institutional Acknowledgements belong to Representative + AcademicPeriod and not to Enrollment.
28. A student may have zero pickups when authorized to leave alone.
29. Each enrollment has at most one submission snapshot for the period.
30. First submission creates the snapshot.
31. Resubmission after reopening replaces the single snapshot.
32. Multiple historical snapshots are not retained in one period.
33. `Student Profile` Bounded Context is removed.
34. Six MVP Bounded Contexts are approved.
35. The MVP remains a modular monolith.
36. Modules integrate through explicit contracts and identifiers.
37. Domain Services are limited to cross-Aggregate rules.
38. Repositories exist per Aggregate Root.
39. Autosave does not generate field-level Domain Events.
40. UserId is the stable technical identity and does not derive from personal identification.
41. A Representative User authenticates with the current Person DocumentNumber as the globally unique LoginIdentifier.
42. Updating that DocumentNumber synchronizes Person and User atomically while preserving User identity, password, status, failed-login state and relationships.
43. A Person with a Representative User must retain complete Identification.
44. Secretaría provisions and administratively replaces Representative credentials; only a secure PasswordHash is persisted and the approved minimum password length is five characters.
45. Representative User requires no activation or mandatory first-login password change; Forgot Password uses the ADR-0020 assisted-recovery model for the MVP, without account lookup or automated reset.
46. Student has no User and no portal in the MVP.
47. FamilyAddress uses MainStreet as its only required textual component; StreetNumber, SecondaryStreet, Sector, Reference and Geolocation are optional, and Geolocation preserves the valid latitude/longitude pair and bounds.
48. FamilyAddress does not contain ProvinceId, CantonId or ParishId and does not consume the geographic catalogs in the MVP.
49. SubmittedAddressSnapshot copies only the address values owned by FamilyAddress and does not contain Province, Canton or Parish codes or names.
50. An AcademicPeriod may have zero or more configurable AcknowledgementRequirements; zero requirements satisfies the stage without an artificial Completion.
51. A Representative confirms the server-resolved active set atomically at most once per AcademicPeriod through one Completion and its individual acknowledgements.
52. A Completion remains valid when requirements are later added, deactivated or have their URL changed; it is never recalculated against the current active set.
53. The SIS stores external URLs only and deliberately keeps no institutional document files, content, hashes or versions.
54. Representative Portal is permanent family self-service; completing Enrollment does not end access or rewrite a Submission snapshot.
55. Secretaría explicitly activates or deactivates AcademicPeriod; zero or one may be ACTIVE, activating another atomically deactivates the previous one, and dates never infer lifecycle.
56. Operational capabilities resolve AcademicPeriod server-side through `GetActiveAcademicPeriod`; Representative never selects it as authority and missing or multiple ACTIVE periods fail closed.

Closely related decisions may be grouped into coherent ADRs without losing traceability.
