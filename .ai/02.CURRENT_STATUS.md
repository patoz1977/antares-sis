## Current Objective

Complete the formal consolidation of the Enrollment Domain in order to establish a stable and consistent business model before resuming software implementation.

The current work is focused on:

- consolidating business entities;
- consolidating business terminology;
- validating aggregate boundaries;
- reviewing relationship types;
- eliminating inconsistencies across the domain documentation.

## Current Risks

- Pending business decisions affecting the domain model.

- Premature implementation could introduce unnecessary technical debt.
