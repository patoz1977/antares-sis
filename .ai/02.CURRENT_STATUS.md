## Current Risks

- The destructive authorization is valid only while the target remains pre-production and disposable; it must not be applied to an institutional or production database containing real data.
- The clean migration sequence is destructive by design under ADR-0018 and must never be pointed at an institutional or production database.
- The 35-table functional inventory, 58-FK baseline and documented-column parity pass locally and on the explicitly authorized disposable MariaDB environment. Physical observation confirmed 36 base tables including `migrations` and 58 FKs.
- Official catalog values not yet supplied remain a data-master concern, not a schema decision. Person forms fail closed when the required active sex and GENERAL_STATUS options are unavailable.
- The temporary exact `admin` gate is intentionally not the definitive institutional role model; broader authorization still requires a separately approved milestone.
- The zero-or-one ACTIVE AcademicPeriod invariant is transactional because the current schema has no clean portable declarative constraint based on a referenced status code. Approved Application transitions serialize on GENERAL_STATUS/ACTIVE and `findActive()` rejects multiplicity, but direct external writes that bypass this boundary remain detectable invalid state.
- ADR-0024 accepts for the MVP the rare administrative deadlock risk between concurrent AcademicPeriod lifecycle and ACTIVE Requirement creation. InnoDB may roll back one transaction, which the administrator can repeat. The risk is known and accepted, not resolved; it does not relax acknowledgement, authorization or atomicity invariants and must be reconsidered if observed operationally or if administrative concurrency increases.
- No direct Representative–Student association exists or is planned. Family is the grouping unit, with Representative membership owned by FamilyRepresentative and Student membership owned by FamilyStudent.
