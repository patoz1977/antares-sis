# Project Status

## Overall Status

The Enrollment Domain Discovery is closed and the AUDIT-001 documentation reconstruction is technically approved as the official Enrollment baseline.

E004.1 — Identity & Access Baseline is completed and merged into `main` through PR #3.

E005 — Person Baseline is the current functional milestone. Phase 1 established ADR-0018 and the pre-production rebaseline boundary. Phase 2 completed the IdentityAccess persistence alignment, the general schema rebaseline and the retirement of incompatible legacy Person/Family flows.

Phase 3A now implements the approved Person Domain as an isolated Aggregate Root with its documented Value Objects and invariants. No persistence, application, HTTP or UI layer is included.

IdentityAccess persistence now targets the official `users` structure without `deleted_at` or `locked_until`. Its domain, application, HTTP, session, CSRF and authentication behavior remain unchanged.

## Current Risks

- The destructive authorization is valid only while the target remains pre-production and disposable; it must not be applied to an institutional or production database containing real data.
- The clean migration sequence is destructive by design under ADR-0018 and must never be pointed at an institutional or production database.
- The 36-table functional inventory and all documented columns passed static parity validation; physical MariaDB execution remains unperformed because no explicitly authorized disposable `E0041_DB_*` environment was available.
- Official catalog values not yet supplied remain a data-master concern, not a schema decision.

## Pending Decisions

No additional business decision was introduced by Phase 3A. Catalog existence, institutional identification uniqueness, actor authorization and persistence coordination remain outside the isolated Aggregate and belong to their already documented application or persistence boundaries.
