## Current Risks

- The destructive authorization is valid only while the target remains pre-production and disposable; it must not be applied to an institutional or production database containing real data.
- The clean migration sequence is destructive by design under ADR-0018 and must never be pointed at an institutional or production database.
- The 36-table functional inventory, documented-column parity, Person Persistence and disposable MariaDB 10.4 scenarios passed validation. Future database execution remains restricted to explicitly authorized disposable `E0041_DB_*` environments.
- Official catalog values not yet supplied remain a data-master concern, not a schema decision. Person forms fail closed when the required active sex and GENERAL_STATUS options are unavailable.
- The temporary exact `admin` gate is intentionally not the definitive institutional role model; broader authorization still requires a separately approved milestone.
- No direct Representative–Student association exists or is planned. Family is the grouping unit, with Representative membership owned by FamilyRepresentative and Student membership owned by FamilyStudent.

## Pending Decisions

The temporary `admin` gate protects the implemented delivery boundary and does not define the definitive role model. Authorization for Secretaría, DECE and Enfermería remains pending a separately approved milestone outside E007.
