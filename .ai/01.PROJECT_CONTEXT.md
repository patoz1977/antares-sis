# Product Vision

Antares SIS is a commercial School Information System (SIS) designed as a reusable software product rather than a solution for a single educational institution.

Although its first implementation targets Unidad Educativa Antares, every architectural and business decision must support its adoption by different educational organizations without requiring changes to the product core.

The long-term vision is to evolve Antares SIS into a configurable platform capable of serving multiple institutions while maintaining a single, maintainable codebase.

---

# Product Objectives

The project pursues the following permanent objectives:

- provide a modern and maintainable School Information System;
- support the complete student lifecycle;
- reduce administrative workload through digital processes;
- centralize institutional information;
- enable incremental functional growth;
- maintain high software quality throughout the product lifecycle;
- minimize technical debt;
- maximize long-term maintainability and reuse.

---

# Product Scope

Antares SIS is intended to cover the operational needs of educational institutions through independent functional modules.

The initial product scope focuses on the enrollment process and the Representative Portal.

Future product evolution may include additional institutional modules while preserving architectural consistency and modularity.

The definition of specific deliverables, milestones and implementation priorities belongs exclusively to `03.ROADMAP.md`.

## Immediate MVP Functional Scope

The immediate functional scope of the product is limited to the following capabilities:

**Secretaría**

- import or create representatives;
- create User accounts for representatives;
- register students;
- associate representatives and students.

**Representative**

- sign in;
- accept institutional documents;
- update their own information;
- update the information of their students;
- complete the enrollment process.

**Institution**

- consult and issue basic reports.

Capabilities outside this list are not part of the immediate MVP unless they are incorporated through the approved project workflow.

# Target Users

The product is designed for multiple categories of users.

Current and future user groups include:

- institutional administrators;
- administrative staff;
- representatives or parents;
- students;
- teachers;
- school management;
- other authorized institutional users.

Each user interacts with the system according to responsibilities defined by the business domain.

Detailed domain responsibilities are documented in `11.DOMAIN_MODEL.md`.

---

# White Label Philosophy

Antares SIS is developed as a completely White Label product.

No educational institution may appear hard-coded in the product. Institution-specific elements shall be configurable rather than implemented directly in source code, business rules or reusable product assets.

Examples include:

- institutional identity;
- logos;
- visual branding;
- configurable institutional information;
- catalogs and operational parameters where applicable.

Institution names, visual identity and institutional information must be supplied through configuration. The historical project name remains Antares SIS and does not authorize coupling the product to a specific institution.

Business logic, architecture and framework components must remain institution-independent.

---

# Multi-Institution Strategy

The system is designed to support multiple educational institutions without requiring separate codebases.

This principle influences all long-term product decisions.

The product supports multiple institutions through a shared codebase. The data-isolation and deployment strategy is defined exclusively in `10.ARCHITECTURE.md` and the corresponding ADRs.

The product shall prioritize:

- institutional independence;
- configuration over customization;
- reusable business components;
- modular growth;
- backward compatibility whenever reasonably possible.

Implementation mechanisms supporting this strategy are defined in the architecture documentation.

---

# Long-Term Goals

The long-term evolution of the product includes:

- consolidation as a commercial SIS product;
- support for multiple educational institutions;
- continuous functional expansion through independent modules;
- cloud-ready deployment capabilities;
- future evolution toward a Software as a Service (SaaS) platform.

These goals describe the strategic direction of the product and do not define implementation priorities.

---

# Guiding Principles

Every project decision should support the following principles:

- business needs drive technical implementation;
- functional delivery is prioritized over technical sophistication;
- unnecessary infrastructure is avoided;
- layers that do not provide direct value to the MVP are avoided;
- simplicity is preferred over unnecessary complexity;
- consistency is preferred over isolated optimizations;
- modularity enables sustainable growth;
- reuse is preferred over duplication;
- documentation evolves together with the software;
- long-term maintainability has priority over short-term implementation speed;
- GitHub is the single source of truth for the project.
